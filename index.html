Interactive Learning

Try Your First Mandarin Word Right Now

Learning characters doesn't have to be intimidating! Use our interactive tool below to learn the structure, traditional vs simplified writing, and core meaning of helpful phrases.

Topic: Essential Travel & Everyday Expressions
你好

Nǐ hǎo

English Meaning
Hello (Literally: "You Good")
Pronunciation Tip: Both characters are in the 3rd tone (dipping). When two 3rd tones sit next to each other, the first automatically changes to a 2nd rising tone.
