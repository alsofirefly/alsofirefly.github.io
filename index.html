Meet Your Teacher

Empathetic, Fun, and Result-Oriented Mandarin Teaching

Hello! I am I-Ying Cheng (鄭亦瑩). I believe that learning a language should never be about memorizing tedious lists of dry characters. It is about connection, understanding cultures, and finding your own voice.

Whether you are a complete beginner trying to grasp tones for the first time, a business professional looking to communicate in high-stakes environments, or looking for AP Chinese prep, I design customized paths that align directly with your lifestyle.

Bilingual Teaching: Clear explanations in English/Mandarin for all grammar complexities.

Customized Materials: Handouts, worksheets, and interactive flashcards crafted by me for you.

Culture Integration: Understand the culture, idioms, and historical context of the words you speak.

Try Your First Mandarin Word Right Now

Learning characters doesn't have to be intimidating! Use our interactive tool below to learn the structure, traditional vs simplified writing, and core meaning of helpful phrases.

Topic: Essential Travel & Everyday Expressions
你好

Nǐ hǎo

English Meaning
Hello (Literally: "You Good")
Pronunciation Tip: Both characters are in the 3rd tone (dipping). When two 3rd tones sit next to each other, the first automatically changes to a 2nd rising tone.
