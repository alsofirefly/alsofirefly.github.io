Lattern Riddles

English riddles about Chinese characters.

Solving lantern riddles (猜燈謎) is a beloved cultural activity during Taiwan's Lantern Festival, held on the fifteenth day of the lunar new year. People gather at festivals to read clever clues on colorful lanterns, testing their wits. Taiwanese versions uniquely blend classical poetry with local wordplay, geography, and pop culture, adding community spirit to the holiday.

Riddle 1

What Chinese character am I?

I have a frame like a window, but I am not a house. I can stand for the center of a city, the middle of a story, or the idea of "just right." What character am I?

Hint

Look for the character that feels balanced and perfectly centered, like something placed exactly in the middle.

Answer

中

zhōng

It means middle, center, or inside. It is a simple placeholder character for this riddle page.
